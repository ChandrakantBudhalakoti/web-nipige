A multi-vendor food delivery app lets many restaurants sell through one branded marketplace, with a customer app to order, a vendor app per restaurant, an admin console, and usually a driver app. Building custom is expensive; Nipige launches a branded multi-vendor food app without code in about 14 days.

A single-restaurant ordering app is a solved problem — plenty of tools do it (see our guide on [on-demand food delivery app development](/blogs/on-demand-food-delivery-app-development) if that's what you're building). A **multi-vendor food delivery app** is a different animal: many restaurants, many drivers, and you in the middle taking a commission on every order. That middle position is the whole business, and it's also where a multi-vendor app gets hard. Here's what one actually needs, and the fastest way to run one.

## What makes an app "multi-vendor"

Single-vendor: one restaurant, its own menu, its own orders. Multi-vendor: dozens of restaurants under **one consumer app**, each with isolated data, their own menu and hours, their own payouts — while customers browse them all in a single cart-and-checkout flow.

That difference forces three things a single-restaurant app never deals with:

- **Vendor isolation** — each restaurant sees only its own orders, menu, and numbers. Restaurant A can't see Restaurant B's data.
- **Split payouts** — every order has to divide automatically between your commission and the vendor's share, with a clear record of who's owed what.
- **Shared dispatch** — one pool of drivers serving all vendors, assigned live by location, not per-restaurant.

Get those three right and you have a marketplace. Miss them and you have a demo that falls over on day two.

## What a multi-vendor food delivery app needs, feature by feature

- **A branded customer app** where people browse every vendor and order across them.
- **A vendor/restaurant panel** — menu management, order processing, availability controls, and each vendor's own analytics.
- **A driver app** with real-time GPS dispatch, route optimization, and delivery OTP — one driver pool, all vendors.
- **A commission and payout engine** — set your cut, split each order, track payouts.
- **An admin console** over the whole marketplace: every order, vendor, driver, and payout in one place.
